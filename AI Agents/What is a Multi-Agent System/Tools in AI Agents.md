# Tools in AI Agents

*TMLC Academy*

---

AI agents are revolutionizing the way we interact with technology. From answering queries to automating workflows, their capabilities are astonishing—but what truly empowers these agents? The secret lies in the tools they utilize. In this article, we'll understand AI Agents tools. Tools can be divided into two categories: **standard tools**, and **custom tools**,

Let's dive in!

---

## 1. Standard Tools: Plug-and-Play Functionality

Libraries provide a suite of standard tools that enhance the utility of Agents across various domains. These tools provide predefined functionalities that can handle specific tasks efficiently. Some prominent examples include:

- **Tavily**: A powerful library for contextualized information retrieval, helping agents provide precise answers based on user queries.
- **Wikipedia API**: Enables agents to fetch information directly from Wikipedia, offering a vast knowledge base at their fingertips.
- **SerpAPI**: Facilitates web scraping from search engines like Google, giving agents access to the latest search results.
- **Math Tool**: Equips agents to perform complex calculations, from solving equations to handling statistical analyses.

These tools act as the building blocks for AI agents, allowing them to perform tasks like fact-checking, data retrieval, and basic problem-solving with minimal customization.

---

## 2. Custom Tools: Tailored for Specific Needs

While standard tools offer general capabilities, custom tools take AI agents to the next level by catering to unique requirements. These tools are often designed or integrated based on specific use cases, providing a higher degree of flexibility and personalization. Examples include:

- **Custom Functions**: Developers can create functions to address unique business needs. For instance, a function that predicts stock prices based on historical data or extracts keywords from niche documents.
- **Data Scraping Pipelines**: Tailored scraping tools allow agents to gather information from specialized websites or platforms, ensuring relevance and accuracy in domains like e-commerce or academic research.
- **API Integrations**: By connecting to proprietary APIs, agents can interact with internal systems, such as CRM platforms or IoT devices, to provide actionable insights and control.

Custom tools transform AI agents into domain experts, capable of delivering specialized solutions that standard tools cannot achieve.

---

## Conclusion

AI agents rely on tools to expand their capabilities and serve users effectively. Standard tools like Tavily and SerpAPI provide robust foundational capabilities, while custom tools unlock tailored solutions for specialized needs. By creatively integrating these resources, developers can craft AI agents that are not just intelligent but also adaptable and resourceful.
