At k = 1, this exact formula reproduces Qdrant's real fused scores from your notebook: 1.0, 0.6667, 0.45, 0.45, 0.3333 — verified, not approximated. Notice RRF never looks at 0.8139 or 5.4881 (the raw dense/sparse scores) — only each document's position in each list. That's the whole trick: it lets you fairly combine two scoring systems on completely different scales (bounded cosine similarity vs. unbounded BM25 sums) without one dominating just because its numbers are bigger.
